# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
B2B buyers, tea distributors, and tea shop owners seeking high-quality tea products.

## Product Purpose
Introduce the Nam Dương Tea brand, present a catalog of tea products, and generate B2B leads through contact and quote forms.

## Brand Personality
Organic, Warm, Approachable

## Anti-references
Not like a generic SaaS template. It should avoid feeling overly clinical, tech-focused, or detached.

## Design Principles
- Practice what you preach
- Show, don't tell
- Cultivate a warm, inviting atmosphere

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure WCAG AA compliance, focusing on good contrast for readability and clear focus states for form navigation.
